How We Score Lawmakers6 Composite Accountability Indices

Scores represent the percentage of substantive roll-call votes where a member supported market-oriented, traditional, or expansionary policies within that dimension. Each score carries an explicit sample size badge (n).

Economic & Fiscal

Measures votes on federal spending reductions, deregulation, corporate and broad-based tax reductions, balanced budget amendments, and debt limit conditionality.

Social Rights & Liberties

Synthesizes voting on civil rights statutes, voting protections, personal autonomy, reproductive healthcare access, Title IX interpretations, and Second Amendment rights.

Government & Oversight

Tracks votes on Congressional stock-trade bans, member compensation freezes, ethics reforms, inspector general independence, Congressional subpoena enforcement, and judicial restructuring.

Security & Foreign Policy

Analyzes National Defense Authorization acts, foreign military and humanitarian aid packages, border security infrastructure, FISA intelligence authorities, and veterans' healthcare funding.

Energy, Tech & Agriculture

Tracks domestic oil, gas, and renewable energy leasing, agricultural commodity programs, environmental review timelines under NEPA, and artificial intelligence / digital platform consumer privacy regulations.

Attendance After ElectionsPost-Election Lame-Duck Rules

To prevent misleading data that penalizes members for medical leaves or rewards post-election absenteeism, Votalume implements strict separation of attendance:

  • Regular-Term Attendance %: Calculated strictly across roll calls occurring during pre-election regular sessions, excluding periods when a member was not yet sworn in or had formally resigned.
  • Lame-Duck Attendance % (Nov–Jan): Computed independently for votes occurring after the general election until the inauguration of the subsequent Congress.
  • "Lame-Duck Drop-Off" Badge: Awarded automatically if a member's attendance during the post-election lame-duck session drops by ≥20 percentage points compared to their regular session baseline.
  • "Served Through Final Bell" Badge: Awarded to departing members who maintain ≥90% attendance during their post-election session.
  • "Recent Absence Alert" Badge: Triggered whenever a lawmaker's attendance across their most recent 20 recorded roll calls falls below 75%.

Served in House & SenateCross-Chamber Career Record

When a Representative is elected to the Senate (e.g. Jim Banks, Adam Schiff, Elissa Slotkin, John Curtis), traditional databases often generate two separate profiles or report false "Not In Office" absences. Votalume unifies their career by Bioguide ID, allowing citizens to view one continuous tenure with separate chamber filter tabs and verified zero-absence transition windows.

5. Financial Disclosure & Sector Overlap Guidelines

Votalume adheres to the following principles to maintain nonpartisan financial reporting:

  • Diversified Funds Exclusion: Broad-market index funds, ETFs, mutual funds, and federally certified qualified blind trusts are explicitly recognized and excluded from individual equity overlap tallies.
  • STOCK Act Disclosures: 30-day transaction notifications are checked directly against periodic transaction reports (PTRs) filed with the House Clerk and Senate Ethics Committee.
  • Raw Overlap Tallies: We report factual counts of supportive vs. restrictive votes cast on legislation impacting sectors in which a member or their spouse holds individual equity.

7. Nonpartisan Guarantee & Discrepancy Audits

Votalume operates strictly under an open civic charter. We accept no funding from candidates, political parties, super PACs, or 501(c)(4) advocacy organizations. All data points are verifiable against primary public records published by the Library of Congress (Congress.gov), the Clerk of the House, and the Secretary of the Senate.
