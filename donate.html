Help Keep Congressional Votes Simple, Fast, and Free for Everyone

I'm a dad of two kids with special needs—my youngest has cerebral palsy—building Votalume in my spare time with no political party, PAC, or outside organization behind it. I started this project to shine a light on who you vote for so you can see whether your representatives actually vote the way you need them to. Everyone deserves an easy way to check if a lawmaker's record matches what matters to their family—and if they aren't voting for what matters to you, you have the facts to vote them out and choose the representative, not just the party.
